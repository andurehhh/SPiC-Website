Lesson 4: Data Privacy Act of 2012

The Shield of Personal Information

Comprehensive Regulation and Protection under Republic Act No. 10173

What is the Data Privacy Act?

The Data Privacy Act of 2012, officially termed Republic Act 10173, is a crucial law in the Philippines that regulates the processing of personal information to protect privacy rights.

Core Objectives of the Act

RA 10173 was established with two primary goals: safeguarding human rights while fostering technological growth.

01

Protect Individual Privacy

The core aim is to safeguard the fundamental human right of privacy in relation to the processing of personal information.

02

Ensure Free Flow of Information

The Act seeks to balance privacy protection with the free flow of information to promote innovation and growth.

Data Privacy Principles

Organizations must adhere to four fundamental principles to ensure the legal and ethical handling of data.

Transparency

Organizations must provide clear and open communication about how they collect, use, and share personal data.

Legitimate Purpose

The collection and processing of personal data must have a specific, appropriate, and lawful purpose.

Proportionality

Only the necessary amount of data should be collected to fulfill the stated purpose.

Accountability

Organizations are responsible for ensuring the security and proper handling of personal data under their control.

Rights of the Data Subject

RA 10173 empowers individuals with specific rights to control their personal information.

Right to be Informed

Knowing how personal data will be processed.

Right to Access

Accessing your own personal data held by organizations.

Right to Object

Stopping processing for direct marketing, profiling, or automated processing.

Right to Rectification

Correcting or updating inaccurate data.

Right to Erasure/Blocking

Requesting the deletion or blocking of personal data.

Right to Data Portability

Receiving personal data in a portable format for your own use.

Understanding Data Processing

Data Processing refers to any operation performed upon personal information, including collection, organization, storage, and destruction.

Key Processing Principles
  • Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency
  • Purpose Limitation
  • Data Minimization
  • Storage Limitation
  • Integrity and Confidentiality
